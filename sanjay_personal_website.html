MY LOVE STORY

A Story That Started in 4th Standard

Some stories don’t begin with a big moment.
They begin with a small feeling.

In 4th standard, I didn’t even know what love meant. But whenever I saw her, there was a special feeling. As the years passed, that feeling slowly grew.

I also had a dream from a young age — to become a director. Cinema was always close to my heart, but her place in my memories became special too.

In 9th standard, I gathered the courage to tell her how I felt. She told me that she saw me only as a good friend. It hurt, but slowly I learned to handle those feelings and move forward.

Later, a few conversations gave me hope again. But eventually I learned that she had feelings for someone else. That moment taught me that we can care deeply for someone, but we cannot control another person’s feelings.

There were more misunderstandings later, including a fake account created in her name that I did not create. I only wish that, one day, she understands that truth.

I don’t need her to accept my love. I don’t need her to come back. I only want the misunderstanding to be cleared.

It isn’t a perfect love story. It isn’t a happy-ending story.
It is simply a real chapter from my life.

And while that chapter remains unfinished, my own story continues — toward my dream of becoming a Director.

— Sanjay
